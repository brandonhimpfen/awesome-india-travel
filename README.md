## Official & Planning

Essential official government portals, tourism boards, e-Visa application platforms, and heritage guides.

- [Incredible India (Official Tourism Portal)](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en) - Official national travel guide featuring destination highlights, cultural itineraries, and festival guides.
- [Ministry of Tourism, Government of India](https://tourism.gov.in/) - Official federal ministry portal providing tourism statistics, sustainable initiatives, and sector regulations.
- [Indian e-Visa Portal](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/) - Official government portal for secure electronic tourist visa applications prior to arrival.

## Digital Payments & Connectivity

Essential digital infrastructure platforms for seamless mobile transactions and communication across India.

- [Unified Payments Interface (UPI / NPCI)](https://www.npci.org.in/) - National digital payments infrastructure enabling instant QR-code mobile transactions via apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm.
- [Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI)](https://www.trai.gov.in/) - Official portal for information on obtaining tourist SIM cards, mobile connectivity, and broadband regulations.

## Transportation

Navigating Indian Railways, domestic aviation networks, metro systems, and long-distance transport.

- [IRCTC (Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation)](https://www.irctc.co.in/) - Official national rail booking platform for passenger trains, luxury tourist trains, and station retiring rooms.
- [Air India](https://www.airindia.com/) - National flag carrier operating domestic routes connecting major metropolitan hubs and regional airports.
- [IndiGo](https://www.goindigo.in/) - Major domestic low-cost airline providing extensive flight coverage across secondary and primary cities.
- [AirSewa (Ministry of Civil Aviation)](https://airsewa.gov.in/) - Official aviation grievance redressal and flight status monitoring portal.

## Destinations & Regions

### Delhi & Golden Triangle

- [Delhi Tourism (DTTDC)](https://www.delhitourism.gov.in/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring Red Fort, Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb, and historic bazaars.
- [Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC)](https://www.delhimetrorail.com/) - Official transit map, token info, and smart card planner for navigating the capital's rapid transit network.

### Rajasthan & Heritage Deserts

- [Rajasthan Tourism](https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/) - Official regional portal for exploring Jaipur's palaces, Udaipur's lakes, Jodhpur's fort, and Thar desert safaris.

### Kerala & Southern Backwaters

- [Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/) - Official visitor hub for Alleppey backwater houseboat cruises, Munnar tea plantations, and Ayurveda wellness retreats.

### Goa & Western Coast

- [Goa Tourism](https://goatourism.gov.in/) - Official coastal portal for exploring heritage Portuguese quarters, spice plantations, and Arabian Sea beaches.

### Himachal Pradesh & Himalayas

- [Himachal Pradesh Tourism (HPTDC)](https://hptdc.in/) - Official regional guide for Shimla, Manali mountain gateways, Rohtang Pass permits, and trekking routes.

### Varanasi & Uttar Pradesh

- [Uttar Pradesh Tourism](https://uptourism.gov.in/) - Official state travel portal for exploring Varanasi's Ganges ghats, Agra's Taj Mahal, and Ayodhya heritage sites.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on 24x7 tourist helplines, police assistance, and emergency contacts.

- [Incredible India 24x7 Tourist Helpline](https://tourism.gov.in/) - Multi-language toll-free tourist support helpline (Dial **1363** or **1800-11-1363**).
- [National Emergency Number (Dial 112)](https://112.gov.in/) - Integrated single emergency response system for police, fire, and health services across India.
